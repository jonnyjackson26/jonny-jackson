Kana Baby is developed by Jonny Jackson. You can delete your account and its data at any time, either in the app or by sending a request on this page.

## In the app (fastest) {#in-the-app}

1. Open Kana Baby and sign in.
2. On the Home screen, tap the **Profile** button (the person icon at the top).
3. Tap the **Settings** button (the gear icon, top right), or tap your account (the first row on Profile).
4. Scroll to **Danger Zone** and tap **Delete Account**.
5. Tap **Delete account** to confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted right away. This can't be undone.

## Without the app {#request}

If you can't use the app anymore, send a request below. Use the same email address or phone number that's on your Kana Baby account so we can find it.

## What is deleted {#what-is-deleted}

- Your account, including your email address or phone number, and the name you shared through Apple or Google sign-in
- Your profile photo
- Your push notification tokens
- Your memberships in families
- Family invites that were sent to you and haven't been accepted
- If you're the last member of a family: that family, plus all of its babies and everything logged for them

## What is kept {#what-is-kept}

- **Entries in shared families.** If other people still use a family, the entries you logged (feedings, sleep, diapers and so on) stay with that family so their history stays complete. They're no longer linked to you.
- **Limited technical data.** Server logs are kept for up to [N] days. Crash reports (Sentry) are kept for up to [N] days. Anonymous usage analytics (PostHog) are kept for up to [N] days and aren't tied to your name, email or phone number.

## How long it takes {#timeframe}

Deleting in the app happens immediately. Requests sent through this page or by email are handled within 30 days. We may reply to confirm the account is yours before deleting it.
